# Privacy Policy for KudoKids

Last updated: October 1, 2026

**KudoKids** ("we", "our", "the application") is committed to protecting the privacy of families and children who use our mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information regarding your use of KudoKids.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

To provide core application features and manage subscriptions, we collect only the following data:

* **Account Information (Parents/Guardians):** Email address and display name provided during sign-in (via Firebase Authentication) to manage the family account and synchronize data across devices.
* **Usage & Profile Data:** Child profile names, custom habits, and rewards created voluntarily by the user for in-app management.
* **Purchase Data & Subscription Identifiers:** Monthly subscription status ($4.99 USD) processed exclusively through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We do not collect or store credit card or financial account numbers.

---

### 2. Children's Privacy (Families Policy Compliance)

KudoKids is designed for family use under direct parent or guardian supervision.
* **We do not directly collect personal identifiable information from children.**
* All child profile data (such as names or daily tasks) is entered and managed solely by the parent or legal guardian.
* The application contains **no advertisements** and uses no advertising tracking SDKs[cite: 3, 7].

---

### 3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information strictly to:
* Provide, maintain, and sync application features across your devices.
* Verify subscription status server-side via RevenueCat and Firebase Cloud Functions.
* Respond to customer support inquiries initiated by the user.

---

### 4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share data only with essential infrastructure providers required to operate the app:
* **Google Firebase (Google LLC):** For user authentication and database storage (Firestore).
* **RevenueCat Inc. & Google Play Billing:** To handle purchase verification and subscription management[cite: 1, 2].

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for commercial or advertising purposes[cite: 3, 7].

---

### 5. Data Security

All data transmitted between the application and our backend servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols (HTTPS).

---

### 6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Users can request full account and data deletion (including all child profiles, habits, and history) at any time by contacting our support email or using the in-app account management options.
